About

제이와이엔은 2022년 10월 인천에서 설립된 온라인 유통 회사입니다. 국내외 패션·라이프스타일 상품을 소싱해 KREAM(크림) 등 온라인 리셀 마켓플레이스에서 판매하며, 운영 업무를 자체 개발한 자동화 도구로 처리합니다. 통신판매업 신고를 마친 사업자이며 외부 투자 없이 자체 자금으로 운영합니다.

JYN is a Korean e-commerce company founded in October 2022 in Incheon. We source fashion and lifestyle products from domestic and overseas retailers and sell them through online channels, mainly the KREAM resale marketplace. We are a registered mail-order business, bootstrapped, and our operations run on automation tools we build in-house.

What we build

  • Inventory and price monitoring across retail sites, with real-time alerts
  • Order, overseas shipping and customs-clearance tracking with automated emails
  • Resale marketplace listing and price management
  • An internal operations dashboard that ties these tools together

We use Claude to design, build and run these tools, and are moving more of the workflow onto the Claude API.
